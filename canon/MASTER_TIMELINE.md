# Master Timeline

> Behind-the-scenes continuity guide. Prose does not need to state every time explicitly.

## Morning setup

### ~8:45–9:15
- Theo and Niko prepare upper deck.
- Theo mentions Benji and Eli's personalities.
- Niko reveals marriage strain with Elena.
- Adrian scolds Theo and Niko for moving too slowly.

### ~9:15–9:25
- Adrian finds Elena making a guest bed.
- They kiss; relationship appears affectionate and mutual.
- Zac calls to say he and Sara are arriving.
- Adrian leaves to greet guests.

### ~9:25–9:40
- Zac and Sara meet Benji and Eli while approaching the port.
- Benji and Eli recognize Sara.
- Zac gently redirects fan talk because Sara wants to unwind.

### ~9:40–9:50
- Boarding.
- Meridian chips distributed.
- Zac hears casino access and refuses his chip.
- Adrian retains it.
- Elena silently catches Adrian's attention behind Zac.
- Adrian smiles/winks toward Elena.
- Sara sees the wink but cannot see Elena and assumes it was directed at Zac.
- Zak arrives alone by limo; Adrian visibly worries that the expected investor is absent.

## Investor / vessel setup

### ~9:50–10:05
- Adrian shows Zak cockpit.
- Timed excursion loop established.
- Auto-navigation, obstacle avoidance, corrective shifts, manual mode, and assisted docking established.
- Boat shift briefly opens a drawer exposing a handgun; Adrian closes it without comment.
- Adrian reveals recent upgrades were financed ahead of expected investment.
- Fleet-expansion ambition established.

### ~10:05–10:15
- Zak calls investor friend.
- Learns friend is backing out of both trip and deal.
- Elena overhears.
- Zak asks Elena not to tell Adrian yet because Zak wants time to find replacement funding.
- Zak later texts Elena: "Please don't tell him yet... I'm trying to fix this... If you tell him now, you're going to make everything worse."
- Message remains unread when Elena later enters storage.

## Elena's final known route

### ~10:10 onward
1. **Zak** — investor confrontation.
2. **Sara** — Elena overhears Sara saying trip is disappointing and she should have gone alone. Sara gets defensive. Benji/Eli overhear only the loudest part. Elena's disco-ball charm breaks into Sara's bag unnoticed.
3. **Theo** — Elena criticizes disorganized/unsafe fitness setup. Theo reacts defensively. Benji hears enough to know Theo was angry.
4. **Niko** — kitchen argument about dirty dishes, marriage tension, and Niko's unwashed favorite chef jacket. Elena tells him to wear a spare; he refuses and wears the dirty favorite jacket with old spaghetti stain.
5. Elena leaves toward storage/service area.
6. Narrative stops following Elena.

## Eli / Adrian delay

### ~10:15–10:20
- Eli complains about gaming-time limit.
- Benji approves transfer of unused time.
- Adrian rushes the admin change while trying to get elsewhere.
- Transfer appears successful but does not save.
- Eli later uses Benji's physical Meridian chip while staying logged into Eli's personal gaming account.

## Murder window

### Shortly after ~10:20–10:25
- Elena meets Adrian in storage.
- She reveals investor is gone.
- She ends affair.
- She intends to confess to Niko, try to repair marriage, and leave Meridian.
- Adrian grabs her as she turns to leave.
- Vessel correction contributes to loss of balance.
- Elena strikes fixed service equipment and dies.

## Immediate cover-up

- Adrian checks Elena; blood gets on his captain shirt.
- Zac's refused Meridian chip falls from Adrian's pocket near Elena unnoticed.
- Theo radios Adrian about a needed tool.
- Adrian lies: "I'm in Niko's kitchen."
- Adrian grabs clean spare chef jacket to cover blood.
- Uses camera-covered guest corridor rather than passing Niko in kitchen.
- Camera records obscured figure in clean white chef jacket.
- Adrian disposes of Elena's credential under/into Theo's room, believing it empty.
- Benji is inside looking for snacks for Sara and mistakes the credential for his own being returned by Eli.
- Adrian reaches captain's quarters, changes into clean shirt, bundles bloody shirt inside spare chef jacket.
- Uses service access to stuff bundle into trash chute.
- Returns upstairs clean and speaks with Sara; Zac joins.

## Theo discovers Elena

- Theo still needs tool from storage.
- Passes kitchen and sees Niko still there; no Adrian.
- Finds Elena in storage.
- Tries to help and gets blood on fitness jacket.
- Sees Meridian chip near Elena.
- Panics because Benji heard earlier argument.
- Hides bloody fitness jacket in trash chute; it catches on Adrian's bundle beneath it.
- Returns and calls Adrian.
- Adrian arrives and scans chip: "It's assigned to Zac."
- Theo notices unread pleading message on Elena's phone, but sender is not yet established in the first interpretation.

## Initial investigation

- Zak message, Eli gaming discrepancy, and Zac chip all become immediate leads.
- Eli admits his time transfer failed and he used Benji's chip.
- Benji's credential remains active in game room under Eli's gaming account, largely clearing Eli.
- Question becomes: where is Benji?
- Adrian, Theo, and Eli split to search.
- Sara, Zac, and Niko remain behind questioning Zac's chip and credibility.
- Niko builds theory that Zac made an advance toward Elena and hurt her after rejection.
- Sara adds the boarding wink detail, making Zac's refusal look possibly staged.
- They call the sender from Elena's phone.
- Zak enters with his phone ringing and realizes Elena's phone is calling him.
- Zak is forced to explain investor situation and his pleading text.

## Benji attack

- During search, Benji independently notices Theo's bloody clothing protruding from trash chute.
- Boat shifts; Benji loses balance.
- Adrian finds him investigating, incapacitates him from behind, moves him away.
- Before Adrian can return for evidence, Eli approaches.
- Adrian and Eli appear to discover Benji together.
- Benji is moved somewhere safe.
- Adrian announces nobody should move alone.
- This prevents Adrian from easily returning to the chute.

## Camera / suspicion shifts

- Eli pushes for camera review.
- Camera shows figure in clean white chef jacket after murder.
- Niko becomes prime suspect despite insisting it is not him.
- Theo remembers Adrian saying he was in Niko's kitchen; this initially sounds like partial support for Niko but does not fully clear him.
- Niko admits marriage frustration and suspicion Elena might have been seeing someone.

## Benji wakes

- Benji says he was intentionally put out from behind.
- Eli associates ability to incapacitate someone with Sara's combat background.
- Suspicion shifts to Sara.
- Eli searches Sara's belongings and finds Elena's distinctive disco-ball charm.
- Niko recognizes it as one he bought Elena.
- Sara reveals her earlier confrontation with Elena.

## Theo / chute chain

- Theo asks Benji why he was near chute.
- Benji says he saw Theo's jacket and believed Theo attacked him to prevent discovery.
- Group recovers Theo's bloody fitness jacket.
- Theo admits hiding it but explains finding Elena and trying to help.
- Evidence supports panic/rescue explanation.
- Theo questions why jacket got stuck after he shoved it down.
- Zac searches deeper and recovers clean spare chef jacket.
- Niko is still wearing dirty favorite jacket with spaghetti stain, proving recovered jacket is not the one he wore.
- Camera case against Niko collapses.
- Adrian's bloody captain shirt remains bundled inside spare jacket unnoticed when placed on evidence table.

## Final pre-reveal

- Other suspect theories weaken:
  - Zak's message context is corroborated.
  - Eli's gaming story is reconciled.
  - Sara's jewelry contact is shown to predate death.
  - Theo's blood is explained.
  - Niko's jacket footage is disproven.
  - Benji was attacked.
  - Zac's chip remains unresolved.
- Group considers whether Elena simply fell.
- Adrian suggests vessel movement could explain an accident.
- Someone points out this does not explain Benji's attack.
- Vessel shifts.
- Niko falls into evidence table.
- Spare chef jacket falls.
- Bloody captain shirt drops from inside.
- "ADRIAN" is embroidered on it.

## Adrian POV / finale

- First Adrian POV reveals missing interval and cover-up.
- He realizes he cannot reach shore as innocent captain and run.
- Heads for cockpit.
- Zak remembers gun drawer and warns: "He's going for his gun!"
- Adrian takes gun, disables auto-navigation, and holds group off.
- Adrian gives his reasoning.
- Niko learns Elena was leaving Adrian and intended to try to repair their marriage.
- Niko lunges.
- Adrian shoots Niko but does not kill him.
- Adrian says: "I can't believe she was gonna leave me for you. I didn't mean to kill her. I couldn't let all of this die with her."
- Sara gets an opening and incapacitates Adrian.
- Zak restores auto-navigation/obstacle avoidance and gets Meridian back on safe course.
- Police meet vessel at dock and take Adrian into custody.
