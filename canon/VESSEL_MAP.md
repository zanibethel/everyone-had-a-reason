# Meridian Vessel Layout

## Core design

Meridian is a small luxury two-level vessel with two circulation systems:

1. **Public/guest routes** — visible, camera-covered corridors and social spaces.
2. **Service routes** — mostly camera-free crew passages linking kitchen, storage, fitness, cabins, captain access, and trash chute.

The mystery depends on the difference between these two circulation systems.

## Upper level

### Cockpit / bridge
- At the bow.
- Access from both public/front side and service/crew side.
- Contains automated navigation controls, manual override, route display, collision avoidance, and assisted docking.
- During Zak's early tour, a normal vessel correction causes a drawer to slide open briefly, exposing a handgun. Adrian closes it without comment.

### Captain's quarters
- Immediately adjacent to cockpit.
- Not directly visible to hallway cameras.
- Has access to both public/cockpit side and private service route.
- Adrian later uses this room to change out of his bloody captain shirt after Elena dies.

### Upper lounge / deck / bar
- Public social area.
- Sara prefers this area for quiet.
- Zac and Zak later talk here about vices.
- Adrian returns here clean after his cover-up and is visibly with Sara and Zac when Theo calls about Elena.

## Lower level

### Guest corridor
- Camera-covered.
- Connects passenger cabins and public activity areas.
- Adrian crosses this corridor wearing a clean spare chef jacket over his bloody shirt after Elena dies, creating the misleading camera image.

### Cabins
- Zac + Sara
- Zak
- Theo
- Benji + Eli

Theo's room matters because Adrian later disposes of Elena's credential under/into the room believing it is empty. Benji is inside looking for snacks for Sara and mistakenly believes the credential is his own being returned by Eli.

### Gaming room
- Public side.
- Eli's Meridian gaming-time limit applies here.
- Eli later uses Benji's physical chip while remaining signed into Eli's personal gaming account.

### Fitness area
- Theo's work area.
- Elena criticizes Theo's disorganization and an unsafe setup here.
- Theo later needs a tool from storage.

### Kitchen
- Niko's work area.
- Niko remains here through the murder window.
- Adrian falsely tells Theo, "I'm in Niko's kitchen," while actually with Elena elsewhere.
- Adrian cannot safely pass Niko here with blood on his shirt, forcing him to use the public camera route instead.

### Storage
- Camera-free service area between/near kitchen and fitness.
- Holds tools, linens, and generic spare chef jackets.
- Elena's fatal confrontation with Adrian occurs here.

### Trash chute
- In camera-free service corridor near storage.
- Adrian stuffs his bloody captain shirt bundled inside the spare chef jacket here.
- Theo later unknowingly jams his bloody fitness jacket on top.
- Benji later notices the protruding bloody clothing and begins investigating.

## Timed route / vessel motion

Meridian runs a pre-programmed excursion loop away from shore and returns to port later that day.

Automated navigation:
- maintains course;
- corrects for wind/current;
- avoids obstacles;
- returns to programmed route;
- assists docking.

Passengers sometimes feel natural corrective shifts during the trip. These are established early and later contribute to:
- Elena losing balance during the fatal confrontation;
- Benji losing balance just before Adrian attacks him;
- Benji waking earlier than Adrian hoped;
- Niko falling into the evidence table and accidentally revealing Adrian's bloody shirt;
- the finale, when Adrian disables automatic navigation and Zak restores it.
