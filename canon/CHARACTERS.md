# Characters

## Zac
Sara's partner and a passenger. Recovering gambling addict. Previously concealed gambling/financial problems, damaging trust. His recovery rule is simple: do not make the first bet. He refuses his Meridian chip because the same credential enables casino access. His arc is accountability without demanding belief or reassurance.

## Sara
Semi-famous competitive kickboxer. Invited by Adrian partly as a high-profile guest to impress the prospective investor. She wants privacy and rest after a recent fight. Her relationship with Zac is strained by past secrecy. Her arc is distinguishing healthy boundaries from avoidance and learning to engage when necessary. Her combat skill later matters in the finale.

## Zak
Musician/networker. His friend was supposed to invest in Adrian's expansion and backs out at the last minute. Zak is accustomed to vouching for people, making introductions, and fixing problems, but rarely feels others show up for him. He drinks when stressed. He sees Adrian as both a client/connection and a potential commission opportunity, while also caring about Niko and Elena as friends. His arc is learning not to absorb everyone else's consequences.

## Theo
Works aboard Meridian managing fitness/recreation. Takes criticism personally and ties mistakes to his worth. Invited brothers Benji and Eli. His arc is admitting mistakes without treating them as proof he is a failure.

## Benji
Theo's brother and passenger. Social, helpful, and uncomfortable saying no. Protects people by withholding truth, even when that creates bigger problems. His arc is learning that loyalty does not mean hiding consequences.

## Eli
Theo's brother and passenger. Gamer who struggles to stop once engaged. His arc is accepting limits and telling embarrassing truths instead of gaming around them.

## Niko
Chef aboard Meridian. Elena's husband. Feels overlooked and struggles to advocate for his needs. His marriage with Elena is strained, but he does not initially know about her affair with Adrian. His arc is trusting his own observations and speaking clearly instead of assuming others should know what he needs.

## Elena
Housekeeping staff aboard Meridian and Niko's wife. Blunt, observant, sometimes intrusive. Secretly involved with Adrian during a rough period in her marriage, but has begun regretting it and ultimately intends to end the affair, tell Niko the truth, try to repair the marriage, and leave Meridian for a new venture away from Adrian. Her death occurs just as she chooses accountability.

Elena wears a charm bracelet with several personally meaningful charms bought by Niko, including a distinctive mirrored disco-ball charm.

## Adrian
Owner/captain of Meridian. Confident entrepreneur, stern boss, overleveraged financially, and obsessed with controlling outcomes. Secretly involved with Elena. He upgraded Meridian on debt expecting Zak's friend's investment to fund expansion. Elena's death is accidental after Adrian grabs her to stop her leaving and a vessel correction contributes to her fall. Everything after that is deliberate cover-up and escalation.
