# Clue Matrix

## Core rule

Every major clue should support both an initial false story and the eventual true story.

| Clue | Initial interpretation | True meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Zac's Meridian chip beside Elena | Zac had contact with Elena near death and may be lying about refusing the chip | Zac refused it; Adrian retained it; it fell from Adrian's pocket while he checked Elena |
| Adrian's boarding wink | Sara thinks Adrian and Zac had a private arrangement after Zac publicly refused the chip | Adrian was acknowledging Elena behind Zac |
| Elena's disco-ball charm in Sara's bag | Sara had physical contact with Elena during/near the fatal event and may be hiding more | Elena's bracelet snagged during an earlier argument; the charm broke into Sara's bag while Elena was still alive |
| Zak's unread text | Pleading/threatening message suggests Zak was pressuring Elena about something secret | Zak was begging Elena not to reveal the investor withdrawal yet so he could try to replace the funding |
| Eli's gaming session ending early | Eli left the gaming room before the murder and cannot account for his location | Adrian's time transfer failed; Eli used Benji's physical chip while staying on Eli's gaming account |
| Benji's credential active in gaming | Benji appears to be in the gaming room | Eli was physically using Benji's credential; credential identity does not equal physical user |
| Theo and Elena arguing | Theo had anger toward Elena shortly before death | Elena criticized a legitimate safety/disorganization issue and Theo took it personally |
| Theo's bloody fitness jacket | Theo attacked Elena, hid evidence, and may have attacked Benji | Theo found Elena, tried to help, got blood on himself, panicked, and hid the jacket |
| White chef jacket on camera | Niko left the area after Elena died | Adrian wore a generic spare chef jacket to hide blood while crossing the camera-covered guest corridor |
| Niko's refusal to wear spares | Petty marital argument | Later proves the camera jacket is not the dirty favorite jacket Niko wore all day |
| Elena's credential in/near Theo's room | Benji/Theo may have taken or planted Elena's access credential | Adrian discarded it while escaping; Benji mistook it for his own chip being returned by Eli |
| Adrian repeatedly checking return time | Concerned captain waiting for police to take over | Adrian is counting down until docking, hoping to delay discovery of his role and escape in shore-side confusion |
| Vessel corrective shifts | Ordinary automated-navigation behavior | Contribute causally to Elena's fall, Benji's imbalance, Benji waking early, and the final evidence-table reveal |
| Gun glimpsed in cockpit drawer | Incidental captain security/safety detail | Zak remembers it when Adrian runs for the cockpit and realizes Adrian is going for the gun |
| Adrian being with Sara/Zac when Theo calls | Apparent alibi | Elena was already dead; Adrian had completed the first cover-up and returned upstairs |
| Adrian saying he was in Niko's kitchen | Initially a forgettable answer to Theo | Direct lie that later contradicts Niko's continuous presence in the kitchen |

## Theme

The evidence is usually real. The error is the story attached to it.
