# Murder Truth

## What actually happens

Elena reaches storage after a morning of interactions that create plausible motives around multiple people.

Adrian meets her there.

Elena tells him:
- Zak's investor friend has backed out;
- Adrian's expected funding is gone;
- she is ending the affair;
- she regrets betraying Niko;
- she intends to tell Niko the truth;
- she wants to try to repair the marriage;
- she wants to leave Meridian and pursue a new venture away from Adrian.

Adrian experiences simultaneous losses:
- investor;
- expansion plan;
- debt solution;
- Elena;
- potentially Niko as staff;
- status/control.

Elena turns to leave.

Adrian grabs her to stop the conversation from ending. Meridian makes one of its normal corrective shifts. Elena pulls away, loses balance, strikes fixed service equipment, and dies.

The death is not premeditated.

Adrian's grab causes the fatal accident.

Everything after that is deliberate concealment.

## Immediate cover-up

Adrian checks Elena and gets blood on his captain shirt.

Zac's previously refused Meridian chip, still in Adrian's possession, falls near Elena without Adrian noticing.

Theo radios Adrian asking for help/tool information. Adrian lies: "I'm in Niko's kitchen."

Adrian cannot pass the kitchen with blood visible because Niko is there.

He grabs a clean generic spare chef jacket from storage and wears it over the bloody shirt.

He crosses the camera-covered guest corridor with his face obscured. The white jacket later appears to implicate Niko.

Adrian gets Elena's credential off his person by sliding/dropping it under/into Theo's room, believing it empty. Benji is inside and mistakes it for his own credential being returned by Eli.

Adrian reaches captain's quarters, changes into a clean shirt, bundles his bloody captain shirt inside the spare chef jacket, exits through service access, and stuffs the bundle into the trash chute.

He returns upstairs clean and calm, speaking with Sara and then Zac before Theo reports finding Elena.

## Theo's accidental preservation of evidence

Theo later goes to storage for the tool, finds Elena, checks her, and gets blood on his fitness jacket.

Because Benji overheard Theo arguing with Elena earlier, Theo panics and hides the bloody fitness jacket in the trash chute.

It catches on Adrian's bundled clothing underneath.

Theo returns to Elena and calls Adrian.

This attempt to hide Theo's own embarrassing behavior physically preserves Adrian's evidence.

## Benji attack

Later, the investigation asks where Benji was during the murder window.

Adrian, Theo, and Eli split up to search for him while Sara, Zac, and Niko remain discussing Zac's credibility.

Benji independently notices bloody clothing protruding from the trash chute and begins investigating.

The vessel shifts; Benji loses balance.

Adrian finds him at that moment and realizes Benji may uncover the bloody captain shirt.

Adrian incapacitates Benji from behind and moves him away from the chute.

Before Adrian can return and retrieve the evidence, Eli approaches. Adrian pivots into appearing to discover Benji at essentially the same time as Eli.

Adrian helps Eli move Benji somewhere safe and later declares that nobody should move alone, unintentionally trapping himself away from his own evidence.

## Chute reveal layers

Benji wakes earlier than Adrian hoped and reveals that he was intentionally attacked and had been investigating blood in the chute.

First recovered: Theo's bloody fitness jacket.

Theo confesses his role, substantially clearing himself, then questions why the jacket got stuck.

Zac checks deeper and finds the clean generic spare chef jacket.

Niko is still wearing his dirty favorite chef jacket with the old spaghetti stain and has been wearing it all day. The recovered jacket therefore cannot be the jacket Niko wore.

This collapses the camera case against Niko and establishes that someone wore a spare.

Adrian's bloody captain shirt remains bundled inside the spare jacket unnoticed when the jacket is placed on the evidence table.

## Final physical reveal

After most apparent suspects are weakened or cleared, Adrian suggests Elena may simply have fallen because the vessel has been shifting all day.

Someone points out that an accident does not explain Benji's attack.

The vessel shifts again.

Niko loses balance, hits the evidence table, and knocks the spare chef jacket down.

Adrian's bloody captain shirt falls from inside it.

His name is embroidered on the shirt.

This is the first undeniable physical reveal against Adrian.

## Finale

After the shirt reveal, the story enters Adrian's POV for the first time and reveals the missing interval and cover-up.

Adrian realizes he can no longer reach shore as the concerned captain and escape in the confusion.

He runs toward the cockpit.

Zak remembers seeing the handgun in a cockpit drawer during the early tour and warns everyone that Adrian is going for his gun.

Adrian gets the gun and takes control of the cockpit while disabling automatic navigation.

He tries to justify what happened.

Niko learns Adrian was Elena's affair partner and that Elena had intended to leave Adrian and try to repair her marriage with Niko.

Niko lunges in anger.

Adrian shoots Niko, wounding but not killing him.

Adrian says variations of:
- "I can't believe she was gonna leave me for you."
- "I didn't mean to kill her."
- "I couldn't let all of this die with her."

Sara later gets a realistic opening to incapacitate Adrian using her established combat skill.

Zak restores the automated navigation Adrian demonstrated earlier. The system corrects course and guides Meridian back toward safe docking.

Police take Adrian into custody.

## Moral distinction

Elena's death begins as an unintended consequence of Adrian's controlling act.

The cover-up, attack on Benji, shooting of Niko, and attempted destruction/crash are conscious escalations.

The central contrast is that every other character eventually admits an embarrassing truth; Adrian keeps choosing one more concealment.
