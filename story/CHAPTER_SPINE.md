# Chapter Spine

> Working POV structure. Suspicion follows pressure, not one-suspect-per-chapter neatness. Adrian receives no POV until the physical shirt reveal.

## Act I — Before anyone knows there is a murder

1. **Theo — Before the Guests**  
   Theo/Niko deck prep; brothers seeded; marriage trouble; Adrian's controlling boss behavior.

2. **Elena — Careful**  
   Adrian/Elena affair revealed to reader through affectionate cabin encounter; Zac arrival call interrupts.

3. **Sara — Not Working**  
   Port walk; meets Benji/Eli; fan fatigue; Zac gently redirects and respects her space.

4. **Zac — Didn't Need One**  
   Boarding; Meridian chips; Zac quietly refuses casino-enabled chip; Sara misreads Adrian's wink; Zak arrives alone and worries Adrian.

5. **Zak — You Could Drive It Yourself**  
   Cockpit tour; timed excursion loop; auto-nav; corrective shifts; manual override; debt; fleet ambition; gun drawer briefly exposed.

6. **Zak — Make Everything Worse**  
   Investor backs out; Elena overhears; Zak asks for time to find replacement funding; threatening-looking pleading text remains unread later.

7. **Sara — None of Your Business**  
   Sara mutters she should have traveled alone; Elena overhears; argument; Benji/Eli hear worst fragment; disco-ball charm breaks into Sara's bag.

8. **Theo — Put It Back Where It Goes**  
   Elena criticizes fitness setup; Benji hears argument; Theo needs tool; Adrian tells him, "I'm in Niko's kitchen."

9. **Eli — Five More Minutes**  
   Eli complains about gaming time; Adrian rushes transfer; it fails; Eli later uses Benji's physical chip.

10. **Niko — The Jacket**  
    Kitchen frustration; dirty favorite chef jacket; Elena tells him to wear a spare; deeper marriage grievance; Elena leaves toward storage and disappears from narrative.

11. **Zak — Nobody Answers**  
    Zak tries replacement investors, gets rejected, talks with Zac at bar about vices and always being the fixer.

12. **Sara — Everything Good?**  
    Adrian returns clean; Sara calls Zac over; Adrian appears normal; Theo calls saying something is wrong with Elena.

## Act II — Discovery and first accusations

13. **Theo — Storage**  
    Backtrack: Theo goes for tool, sees Niko still in kitchen, finds Elena, gets blood on jacket, hides it in chute, sees Zac chip, calls Adrian; unread text preview on Elena phone.

14. **Eli — It Didn't Work**  
    Gaming discrepancy examined; Eli admits transfer failed and he used Benji's chip; Benji credential remains active under Eli's account; question becomes where Benji is.

15. **Zac — What Was She Going to Tell Me?**  
    Adrian/Theo/Eli leave to search for Benji. Sara, Zac, Niko remain. Niko accuses Zac based on chip and mysterious text; Sara adds wink detail; they call the text sender.

16. **Zak — It Was About the Deal**  
    Zak enters with phone ringing from Elena's phone; suspicion pivots to him; he explains investor withdrawal and his plea for time.

17. **Eli — Benji**  
    Backtrack: Eli searches; Adrian and Eli appear to discover unconscious Benji together; they move him and return.

18. **Niko — The Man in White**  
    Camera review at Eli's urging shows clean white chef jacket; Niko becomes prime suspect; he admits marriage frustration and suspected infidelity.

19. **Benji — Something Behind Me**  
    Benji wakes; says he was intentionally put out from behind; no attacker seen; suspicion begins to shift toward Sara's combat ability.

20. **Sara — Elena's Jewelry**  
    Eli searches Sara's bag and finds Elena's distinctive disco-ball charm; Niko recognizes it; Sara explains argument and accidental snag.

21. **Benji — I'm Not Covering for You**  
    Benji reveals he was investigating Theo's bloody jacket in chute; accuses Theo of attacking him to stop discovery.

22. **Theo — I Hid It**  
    Theo's bloody jacket recovered; he admits hiding it but explains finding Elena and panicking; then asks why it got stuck.

23. **Zac — Underneath**  
    Zac searches chute deeper, finds clean spare chef jacket; Niko's dirty favorite jacket proves camera garment was a spare. Adrian's bloody shirt remains bundled inside unnoticed.

24. **Zak — What We Actually Know**  
    Group separates assumptions from facts; Zak/Eli theories weaken; credential does not equal physical user becomes explicit principle.

25. **Sara — Maybe Nobody**  
    Sara's jewelry contact is shown to predate murder; major suspect theories weaken; accident possibility emerges.

26. **Niko — An Accident?**  
    Adrian suggests vessel motion could explain Elena's fall; Benji attack remains unexplained; boat shifts; Niko hits evidence table; spare jacket falls; bloody shirt marked ADRIAN drops out. Cut.

## Act III — What actually happened

27. **Adrian — Ten O'Clock**  
    First Adrian POV. Missing interval: investor loss, breakup, Elena's intent to confess/repair marriage/leave Meridian, fatal grab + vessel shift, Zac chip falls.

28. **Adrian — Keep It Together**  
    Cover-up shown: kitchen lie, spare chef jacket, camera corridor, Elena credential disposal, captain quarters, bloody shirt bundle, chute, clean return upstairs.

29. **Adrian — Just Until Shore**  
    Investigation replayed from Adrian's internal perspective; Niko becomes scapegoat; Benji attack revealed; Eli interrupts evidence recovery; buddy rule traps Adrian; shirt reaches evidence table; present catches up.

## Act IV — Control

30. **Sara — Manual**  
    Adrian runs for cockpit. Zak remembers gun and warns everyone. Adrian takes gun, disables auto-nav, tries to justify himself. Niko learns Elena was leaving Adrian and lunges; Adrian shoots Niko nonfatally. Sara gets an opening and incapacitates Adrian.

31. **Zak — Back on Course**  
    Zak restores auto-navigation and collision avoidance from early demonstration; group contributes; Meridian returns to safe route and dock.

32. **Zac / Ensemble — Everyone Had a Reason**  
    Police take Adrian. Emotional aftermath without forced reconciliation. Everyone's smaller truth is owned. Adrian's contrast: one more concealment until collapse.

## Structural rules

- POV follows the character under the most interesting new pressure.
- A chapter should ideally end by handing suspicion or uncertainty to another character.
- Never let a POV character explain a clue they do not yet understand.
- Do not fully exonerate Zac before Adrian POV; the chip remains the last unresolved physical clue.
- Adrian's final POV must connect known objects/locations rather than introduce new mechanisms.
