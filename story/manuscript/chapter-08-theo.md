# CHAPTER 8

## Theo

Theo knew Elena was going to say something before she said it.

She had that look.

Not angry.

Worse.

Observant.

She stood just inside the fitness room with her arms crossed, slowly scanning the floor.

Theo followed her eyes.

Resistance bands.

A pair of dumbbells.

Two foam rollers.

A crate that had not made it into storage yet.

Nothing terrible.

Nothing that needed commentary.

Elena pointed.

“That can’t stay there.”

Theo looked at the crate.

“It’s not staying there.”

“It’s there now.”

“I’m still setting up.”

She stepped around it.

“If somebody comes through here and catches their foot—”

“They won’t.”

Elena looked at him.

“You know that how?”

Theo sighed.

“Because I’m moving it.”

“Then move it.”

He stared at her.

She stared back.

Theo hated that she could make three words sound like a performance review.

“I said I’m working on it.”

“I know.”

“Doesn’t sound like you know.”

Elena glanced toward the equipment rack.

One of the resistance bands was hanging halfway off the side.

She reached for it.

Theo got there first.

“I’ve got it.”

Elena pulled her hand back.

“Okay.”

Theo hung it properly.

Then immediately regretted doing it while she watched.

It felt like proving her point.

Elena looked toward the far wall.

“Those should probably be secured too.”

Theo laughed once.

“Anything else?”

She looked at him.

“You asked.”

“I absolutely did not.”

That almost made her smile.

Almost.

Theo turned away and picked up the crate.

“I’ve been doing this all morning.”

“I know.”

“Then why are you in here checking my work?”

“I’m not checking your work.”

“You walked in and immediately started telling me what’s wrong.”

“I walked in because Adrian asked me to make sure the guest areas were ready.”

“This is my area.”

Elena nodded.

“Okay.”

Theo set the crate down harder than he meant to.

“Then let me handle it.”

Elena's expression changed.

Not much.

But enough.

“I was trying to help.”

“I don't need help.”

The room went quiet.

Theo knew that sounded harsher than he intended.

He also wasn't going to take it back while she was still standing there looking at everything.

Elena nodded once.

“Fine.”

She turned toward the door.

Benji was passing outside.

He slowed when he heard Theo's voice.

Theo saw him.

Great.

Elena stepped into the hall.

Benji looked between them.

“All good?”

“Yep,” Elena said.

Theo didn't answer.

Benji lingered half a second.

Then kept walking.

Theo waited until both of them were gone.

“Everybody's a supervisor today.”

He picked up the crate again.

This time he carried it toward the service door.

The storage room was close, but he stopped halfway there.

He had forgotten the mounting tool.

Theo closed his eyes.

“Of course.”

He set the crate down.

The tool should have been in storage.

Should have been.

But after the maintenance work earlier, Adrian had moved half the stuff around.

Theo pulled the radio from his waistband.

“Adrian?”

Static.

Then:

“Yeah?”

“Where’d you put the driver kit?”

A pause.

Theo looked at the crate sitting in the corridor.

“Adrian?”

“I’m busy in Niko’s kitchen.”

Then silence.

Theo stared at the radio.

“Not helpful.”

He clipped it back onto his waistband.

For the next few minutes, he checked the fitness room again.

Behind the storage bench.

Under the counter.

Inside the equipment cabinet.

Nothing.

He opened the same drawer twice like the tool might have appeared between attempts.

“Come on.”

Theo looked toward the service corridor.

Storage.

It was the obvious place left.

He picked up the crate and headed that way.

The service corridor was quieter than the guest side of the boat.

He reached the storage room and nudged the door open with his shoulder.

Then stopped.

The crate slipped from his hands.

It hit the floor hard.

Theo didn’t move.
