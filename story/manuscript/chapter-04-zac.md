# Chapter 4
## Zac

Zac had barely stepped onto the Meridian before somebody handed him a piece of metal that looked more expensive than half the things he owned.

It was round, slightly larger than a casino chip, with a dark outer ring and the Meridian logo stamped into the center.

He turned it over.

No numbers.

No visible strip.

Just his name and a small embedded light around the edge.

Adrian stood at the top of the boarding steps with a tablet in one hand.

“Those are your access chips,” he said.

Benji held his up.

“For the rooms?”

“Rooms, purchases, activities, gaming.”

Eli perked up immediately.

Adrian noticed.

“And yes, the gaming room.”

Theo appeared behind him and slapped a hand onto Eli’s shoulder.

“Don’t encourage him.”

“I’m not encouraging him.”

“You built the room.”

“That’s different.”

Benji laughed.

Sara stepped onto the deck behind Zac.

Adrian’s attention shifted to her.

“Sara. Glad you made it.”

“Thanks for having us.”

“You need anything while you’re here, ask. Food, space, whatever. This trip is supposed to be easy.”

“Easy sounds good.”

Theo pulled Benji into a quick hug, then Eli.

Eli immediately complained.

“Bro, get off me.”

“You drove all the way out here and that’s the greeting?”

“I said hey.”

“You asked where the gaming room was.”

“That counts.”

Adrian continued explaining the chips.

“Cabin access, purchases, reservations. Gaming time is tied to them too. Casino works the same way.”

Zac looked up.

“Casino?”

“Automated. Blackjack, roulette, a few machines.”

Zac handed the chip back.

“No thanks.”

Adrian looked at him.

“You sure?”

“Yeah.” As Zac looked towards Sara watching.

Adrian took it without another word.

A quick smile crossed his face.

Then a small wink.

Zac never saw it.

Sara did.

Her expression changed.

Zac noticed that instead.

“You okay?”

“Yeah.”

He watched her for half a second.

Then nodded.

“Okay.”

And let it go.

Theo was already trying to explain the layout to his brothers.

“Gaming room’s downstairs.”

“Near our cabin?” Eli asked.

“Kind of.”

“What does kind of mean?”

“It means if you get lost on a boat this size, I’m leaving you there.”

Benji laughed.

A car pulled up near the marina entrance.

Long.

Black.

Polished enough that Eli stopped talking.

“Okay.”

Benji looked toward it.

“Who’s that?”

Adrian stepped closer to the rail.

“That’ll be Zak.”

There was more energy in his voice now.

Theo noticed it too.

“So that’s the guy?”

“His friend is the guy.”

Zac looked at Theo.

“What guy?”

Theo shrugged.

“Money guy, I think.”

The limo stopped.

The rear door opened.

Adrian smiled.

Zak stepped out.

Alone.

Adrian’s smile held for a beat.

Then his eyes shifted toward the open door.

Nobody else got out.

The driver closed it.

Zak started toward the gangway.

Adrian looked past him once more before forcing his attention back.

“Man, good to see you.”

They shook hands.

Zak glanced around.

“Nice setup.”

“Thanks.”

Then Adrian asked the question he had clearly been trying not to ask.

“Where’s your friend?”

Zak’s face changed slightly.

“Uh…”

He glanced back toward the limo pulling away.

“I thought he’d be here.”

Adrian looked toward the road.

Then back at Zak.

The smile stayed.

His eyes didn’t.
